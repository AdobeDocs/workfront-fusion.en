# Workfront Fusion release activity: Week of September 14, 2026

This page describes all enhancements made in Adobe Workfront Fusion the week of September 14, 2026.

For a list of all recent changes, see [Adobe Workfront Fusion release activity](/help/workfront-fusion/fusion-product-releases/fusion-release-activity.md).

For a list of recent bug fixes in Workfront Fusion, see the [Workfront Maintenance Updates](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/workfront-known-issues/releases/current-updates) page and check for any updates labeled Workfront Fusion Maintenance Update.

## Added PKCE connection to the Salesforce connector

You can now authenticate your Salesforce connection using PKCE, in addition to OAuth 2. When you add a Salesforce connection, select your preferred authentication method from the new Connection type dropdown.

For more information, see [Salesforce modules and their fields](/help/workfront-fusion/references/apps-and-modules/third-party-connectors/salesforce-modules.md).
